# Word Online (Business) (cloud connector)

Populate Word templates and convert Word documents to PDF.

This page documents every **cloud connector operations** in this group (2 items).

## Operations

### Convert Word Document to PDF

- **Inventory id:** `word-online/convert-word-document-to-pdf`
- **Kind:** cloud-connector-operation
- **Purpose:** Converts word Document to PDF.

### Populate a Microsoft Word template

- **Inventory id:** `word-online/populate-a-microsoft-word-template`
- **Kind:** cloud-connector-operation
- **Purpose:** Types or fills a Microsoft Word template.
